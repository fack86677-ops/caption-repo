Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the transcription for Hindi / Hinglish? ▼
Harsh Caption Generator uses high-accuracy Whisper AI fine-tuned with Indic transliteration dictionaries. It achieves up to 97% accuracy on Hindi, Hinglish, Urdu, and regional languages.
How does the credit system work? ▼
Credits are charged based on video duration: ≤10s uses ~25-30 credits, ≤30s uses ~50-55 credits, ≤1m uses ~70-80 credits. Every new user gets 100 Free Credits upon sign-in. Additional credit packs start at just ₹60 for 1,000 credits!
Can I edit individual words and customize styles? ▼
Yes! You have full control over font size, colors, highlight colors, black outlines, drop shadows, safe-zones, and 2-3 word chunking. You can also double-click any word in the transcript list to edit it directly.
What formats can I export? ▼
You can export high-quality burned MP4 videos (1080p / 4K), SubRip (.SRT), WebVTT (.VTT), Word Document scripts (.DOCX), plain text (.TXT), and Alpha-Channel green screen videos for Premiere Pro & DaVinci Resolve.
